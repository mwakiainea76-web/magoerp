On the bold weight:

Category headings (Analytics, Assessments, Courses) look like they're at 600/700 weight. Drop to 500 (medium) — still distinguishes them from body text without shouting.
If your font family only ships 400/700 (no 500), that's often why it looks heavy — check if you have a medium weight available; if not, keep 700 but reduce the font-size slightly instead of fighting the weight.

Other small polish items I'd flag:

The "N/N selected" counter and heading are sitting very close in weight/color right now — make sure the counter is clearly lighter (secondary/muted text color) so the eye goes to the heading first, count second.
Selected checkbox rows all look identical in border weight to unselected ones would (once you have unchecked items to compare against) — worth confirming checked vs. unchecked states are visually distinct enough at a glance, not just via the checkbox fill.
Section header row and the divider line above/below are very close in contrast — a touch more spacing (padding) between sections would help them read as separate groups rather than one continuous list.
The chevron icon is quite light gray — fine, but make sure it's clearly clickable (cursor pointer + maybe a hover background on the whole header row).
